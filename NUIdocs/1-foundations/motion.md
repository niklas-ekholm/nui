# Motion

Motion is minimal. No decorative animation.

## Hover reveal

Embed chrome fades in over ~1800ms ease when the pointer enters the embed region. See [[progressive-disclosure]].

## Control feedback

Colour transitions on ghost controls are instant or very short — no bounce or scale.

## Scrubbing

While dragging scrub labels, cursor and body classes may lock interaction; no animated easing on the value itself.
