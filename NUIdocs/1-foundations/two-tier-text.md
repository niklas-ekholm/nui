# Two-Tier Text

#designpattern

**Content** colour for readable emphasis; **ui** colour for muted chrome at rest.

## Purpose

Cross-element behaviour definition — separates note-readable text from workspace chrome.

## Rule

Body copy and primary labels use **content**. Toolbar labels, axis ticks, and metadata use **ui** at rest; hover may promote to **content**.
