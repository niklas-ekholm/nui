The bases that are not tied to a particular folder live here.

- [[Timeline.base]] — projects on a date axis, in three layouts
- [[Tasks.base]] — checkbox tasks from the project notes
- [[Gallery.base]] — picture gallery, and cards at two sizes
- [[Scores.base]] — a numeric property over time

Two bases sit elsewhere, because their views read the folder they are in rather
than a query: [[Contents.base]] at the vault root, and [[Tracker.base]] in
Habits.
